QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Kitchen Engineering FAQs

Where specified in the manufacturer catalogue (such as models CG-8001 and CG-8003), both the internal carcasses and door structures are manufactured using food-grade 304 stainless steel. This alloy offers exceptional corrosion resistance, high tensile strength, and zero susceptibility to moisture or insect rot.

The Gudanni manufacturer catalogue outlines solid stainless worktops in 6 mm and 10 mm thicknesses, finished with a sandblasted anti-scratch surface treatment. Additionally, high-density black quartz stone countertops are specified for several standard models.

The motorized lift basket is installed within upper cabinet modules. It is actuated via a concealed capacitive touch sensor on the underside of the cabinet or an optional wireless remote switch. It descends smoothly to bring condiments, glassware, or appliances to eye level.
