KINO STAINLESS STEEL SERIES

Waterproof Architecture.
Enduring Serenity.

Bathrooms present the most unforgiving humidity, steam, and cleaning chemical environment in any residence. Traditional MDF and plywood vanity units delaminate, swell, and harbor mildew over time. The Kino stainless steel vanity collection solves this permanently.

Each vanity model pairs an all-metal stainless carcass with internal honeycomb-aluminium panels for rigid, warp-free performance. Paired with sintered stone slabs pressed under 400 BAR pressure and fired at ~1200°C, FEALITY vanities provide an impervious architectural statement.

MANUFACTURING DETAIL

Structural Rigor.
Felt in Daily Use.

The Kino manufacturer catalogue details precise architectural construction techniques that set these bathroom vanities apart from standard market units:

Honeycomb-Aluminium: Internal core reinforcement eliminates panel vibration and prevents warping over decades of hot shower steam.
DTC Soft-Close Hinges: Precision hydraulic dampening specified across the 2023 series for silent, frictionless operation.
Pull-Basket Drawers: Ergonomic pull-out basket drawer systems maximize accessible volume beneath bathroom plumbing traps.
400 BAR Sintered Slate: Pressed under 400 BAR pressure and fired at ~1200°C for zero porosity, stain resistance, and scratch immunity.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Vanity Engineering FAQs

Bathrooms experience severe humidity fluctuations. Standard wood-based materials absorb steam, leading to blistered veneer, swollen drawer faces, and fungal odors. Stainless steel carcasses are completely immune to water, mildew, and steam deterioration, lasting decades without degradation.

Stainless steel sheets can create an acoustic 'tinny' sound or flex if unsupported. Kino vanities sandwich an aerospace honeycomb-aluminium core between steel sheets, providing extreme rigidity, deadening acoustic resonance, and preventing thermal warping without adding deadweight.

The Kino supplier catalogue specifies slate tops pressed at 400 BAR pressure and fired at approximately 1200°C. This extreme vitrification creates a dense, non-porous surface that resists cosmetic staining, perfumes, hair dyes, thermal shock, and direct scratches.

Yes. The illuminated mirror units feature integrated ambient LED borders and can be connected directly to your bathroom's electrical wiring. Touch sensors and anti-fog heating pads are supported depending on model selection.
