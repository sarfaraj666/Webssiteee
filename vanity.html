MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE CHOOSING WITH CONFIDENCE

BEYOND THE SURFACE

Steel or wood?
Know the difference.

The right choice depends on where and how a space is used. Steel offers valuable advantages, but neither material is universally best. Here's a clear starting point for the conversation.

WHAT MATTERS STEEL WOOD / WOOD-BASED
Moisture & splashes Suitable stainless grades can resist moisture well. Wood performance depends on sealing and moisture control.
Termites Steel itself is not a food source for termites. Natural wood and some wood-based boards may need protection.
Cleaning Smooth steel finishes are typically easy to wipe clean. Care varies with finish; prolonged moisture can be a concern.
Look & feel Modern lines, paint/coating options and metallic textures. Natural grain, tactile warmth and a broad range of finishes.
Points to consider Grade, coating, dents, scratches, fabrication and cost. Swelling, moisture exposure, pests, finishing and upkeep.

Material performance depends on the exact grade, protective finish, workmanship, installation and maintenance. Discuss product-specific specifications with the seller before purchasing.

GOOD DESIGN STARTS WITH GOOD QUESTIONS

Ask better.
Choose better.

Useful answers about steel, surfaces and daily living.

Why consider steel for a vanity? +

Bathrooms experience humidity and splashes. Correctly selected stainless steel or well-finished metal can perform well in these conditions.

Can bathroom steel rust? +

Yes, unsuitable grades, damaged coatings, standing water or harsh cleaners can cause corrosion. Specification and care are important.

What should I ask before buying? +

Ask about the exact steel grade, thickness, surface treatment, hinges, installation and cleaning instructions. Request product-specific guidance.
